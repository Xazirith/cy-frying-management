## Power Consumption

### Per Display
- Raspberry Pi 4: 3-5W
- 24" Monitor: 20-25W
- **Total: ~30W per display**

### Annual Electricity Cost
- 30W × 24h × 365 days = 262 kWh/year
- At $0.12/kWh = **$31/year per display**

5 displays = $155/year in electricity

## Insurance & Protection

Consider:
- **Surge protectors** - $20-40 each
- **UPS backup** - $100-200 (for critical displays)
- Equipment insurance for valuable installations

## When to Upgrade

**Pi 3 → Pi 4**
- Struggling with video playback
- Need 4K output
- Running hot constantly

**2GB → 4GB RAM**
- Multiple content types
- Complex playlists
- Video content

**4GB → 8GB RAM**
- 4K video
- Web content
- Heavy graphics
